Plague Rat

Every townsperson a rat touches catches the plague and breeds another rat. Go too long without an infection and a rat dies.

Rat hunters carry a number. While your swarm is smaller, he chases you on sight, and if he touches your lead rat it's over. Match his number and he runs, and your rats can eat him for 5 more rats. Eat every hunter to take the district.
